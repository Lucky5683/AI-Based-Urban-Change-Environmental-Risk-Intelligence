# Project Scope & Research Questions

## Date
2026-09-06

## Objective
Establish the foundational scope, geographical domain, temporal boundaries, and primary research questions for the AI-Based Urban Change & Environmental Risk Intelligence initiative in Chittoor District, Andhra Pradesh, India.

## Input Data
- District boundary definitions for Chittoor District, Andhra Pradesh, India.
- Decadal multi-temporal satellite observations (2016 baseline vs. 2025 current).
- Multi-source environmental datasets (satellite imagery, climate, precipitation, land cover, terrain).

## Data Source
- Administrative boundary: Survey of India / GADM / Local Government GIS.
- Earth observation: Copernicus Sentinel-2 (ESA) via Google Earth Engine.
- Built-up & Land cover: ESA WorldCover / Dynamic World / GEE classified products.

## Method
Formulate a multi-hazard, multi-temporal analytical framework focused on:
1. Urban growth and built-up expansion dynamics (2016–2025).
2. Environmental risk intelligence (vegetation degradation, thermal anomaly/UHI, hydrological stress, flood/runoff susceptibility).
3. Machine learning-driven spatial risk modeling and predictive forecasting.

## Results
Defined core research objectives:
- **RQ1 (Urban Morphology)**: What is the spatial magnitude, direction, and rate of built-up expansion in Chittoor District between 2016 and 2025?
- **RQ2 (Environmental Impact)**: How has urban change altered local surface temperature (LST), vegetation cover (NDVI/SAVI), and surface moisture (NDWI/MNDWI)?
- **RQ3 (Multi-Hazard Risk)**: Which mandals and sub-regions face elevated compound environmental risk from urban pressure and climate stress?
- **RQ4 (Predictive Modeling)**: Can machine learning models accurately forecast high-risk environmental zones based on historical land transitions and spatial indicators?

## Validation Status
PASS
Project scope, study region, temporal baseline (2016–2025), and analytical structure are fully aligned.

## Scientific Interpretation
- **Observation**: Chittoor District exhibits rapid peri-urban growth along transport corridors connecting Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka.
- **Inference**: Decadal environmental change requires calibrated multi-spectral indices to separate climatic fluctuations from anthropogenic urban expansion.

## Limitations
- Decadal comparison (2016 vs. 2025) provides two discrete temporal anchor points; intermediate annual fluctuations require longitudinal statistical verification.

## Decision
Proceed with structured data acquisition and rigorous data validation protocols.
